About

Behind every data point in my work is a person whose story deserves to be heard. I bring together data science, human rights and public policy: computational methods to reveal patterns of state violence, and qualitative research to keep survivors' voices at the centre. The goal is evidence that policymakers, journalists and communities can use to demand accountability.

My academic work began at North South University under Dr. Mohammad Monirujjaman Khan, where I contributed to four funded projects and presented papers at IEEE conferences in the United States, Canada, India and Egypt. I have also worked in ICT security at LankaBangla Finance, led a free online school during COVID-19, and founded the dialogue platform Eight Onwards.

I come from a family of educators, freedom fighters and public servants, and I want to use knowledge and technology for a more just, equitable and accountable Bangladesh.
